Classic Reversi is a strategic board game for two players on an 8×8 board.

Features:
- Multiple difficulty levels, including Super Easy on a smaller board
- Play vs computer or local 2-player
- Daily challenge, stats, and achievements
- Themes, sound, and haptics
- Optional hints and undo (rewarded)
- One-time Remove Ads purchase
- How to Play guide

Enjoy timeless Reversi strategy with a clean, modern interface.
