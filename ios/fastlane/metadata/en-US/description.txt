Classic Reversi (also known as Othello) is a strategic board game for two players, played on an 8×8 uncheckered board.

Features:
- Three difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, and Hard.
- Play against the computer or a friend (local 2-player mode).
- Choose who goes first.
- Undo moves.
- Legal move hints on Easy difficulty.
- Track your wins and losses.
- Responsive design for phones and tablets.

Enjoy the timeless strategy of Reversi with a clean and modern interface!
